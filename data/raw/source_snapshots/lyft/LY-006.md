# LY-006 — Pending charges

Company: Lyft
URL: https://help.lyft.com/hc/en-us/all/articles/115012926167-Pending-charges
Date accessed: 2026-06-06
Visible last updated date: Not visible

## Relevant issue families

- pending_or_unexpected_charge

## Source summary

Directly answers the "is a pending Lyft charge an actual charge?" question. Explains that a pending transaction is a temporary authorization, not a final charge. The hold typically disappears or appears as a refund within 5–7 business days depending on the bank. Riders can check Ride history in the app to confirm whether a final charge was applied.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Pending charge is a temporary authorization, not a final charge.
- Drop-off time: 5–7 business days, depending on bank.
- Pending holds may appear as a refund within that window.
- Does not provide universal refund timing for posted-charge refunds.

## User action required

- Wait up to 5–7 business days for the pending hold to drop off.
- Check Ride history in the app to confirm final charges.

## Escalation path

- Routes to Ride history; does not give a strong 'contact support if X' path.

- Strong source for pending-charge questions (Q016).
