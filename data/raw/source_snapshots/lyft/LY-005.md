## Source summary

Explains how riders can estimate the cost of a Lyft ride using the in-app fare estimate before requesting. Notes that the upfront estimate may differ from the final charge due to ride conditions such as route, traffic, and stops. Does not provide a complete dispute policy by itself.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Estimates may differ from final charge for stated ride-condition reasons.
- Does not commit to fare adjustments when estimate differs from final charge.

## User action required

- Use the in-app fare estimate to preview cost.
- Expect possible differences for stated reasons.
