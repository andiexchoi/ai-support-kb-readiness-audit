## Source summary

Describes the in-app ride history tool and includes a brief Lost & found section. The Lost & found section instructs riders to tap 'Find Lost Item' and follow prompts to message the driver directly via masked text. Includes a 'Contact Support' link for lost-phone scenarios. Points to the dedicated LY-004 article for fuller guidance.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Does not state lost-item policy in depth.
- Routes to LY-004 for substantive guidance.

## User action required

1. Open ride history.
2. Tap 'Find Lost Item' for the relevant ride.
3. Follow prompts to message the driver.
4. For lost phones, tap 'Contact Support'.

## Escalation path

- In-app 'Find Lost Item' flow.
- Masked-text contact with driver.
- 'Contact Support' link for lost phones.
