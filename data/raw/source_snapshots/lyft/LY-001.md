## Source summary

Driver-facing help article describing when Lyft drivers are paid cancellation or no-show fees. Covers cancellation timing, rate-card regions, acceptance rate, Lyft Teen rides, scheduled rides, no-show procedure, warnings for excessive no-show cancellations, and reasons a driver may need to cancel.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Driver is paid a cancellation fee if a passenger cancels 1 minute or more after the driver started driving to pick them up.
- In rate-card regions, the driver receives the higher of a minimum cancellation fee or earnings based on time/distance driven.
- For no-show fees, the driver must tap to arrive, wait the timer down to 0:00 without moving from pickup, contact (or be contacted by) the passenger, and cancel via "Passenger didn't show up."
- Driver does not get a no-show fee if they are too far from pickup.
