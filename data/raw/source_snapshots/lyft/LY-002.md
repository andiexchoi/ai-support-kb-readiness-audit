## Source summary

Rider-facing help article that explains pending charges, cancellation fees, no-show fees, and how to dispute a fee in the Lyft app. Says riders may be charged in specific situations and can dispute charges they believe are wrong.

## Relevant policy language summary

- A rider may be charged a cancellation fee if they cancel:
  - More than 30 seconds after a driver accepts the ride
  - More than 30 seconds after requesting a Wait and Save ride
  - After the driver arrives
  - 3 or more rides within a 15-minute timeframe
- A rider can cancel within the first 5 minutes after pickup if someone else took the ride or there's another critical issue.
- For Lyft Scheduled rides, a fee may apply if cancellation is within 1 hour of pickup and a driver was matched, the driver is on the way, or the driver is scheduled to arrive within the pickup window.
- No-show fee may apply when the driver arrived, waited the required time, and attempted contact.
- Pending charges after a canceled ride may be temporary authorization holds that disappear or show as a refund within 5–7 business days.
- The article does **not** explicitly state that a stationary or non-progressing driver makes the rider eligible for a refund.

## User action required

To dispute a cancellation or no-show fee in the Lyft app:

1. Select your profile.
2. Under "Account," select "Ride history."
3. Select the ride you need help with.
4. Select "Dispute ride charge" at the bottom of the screen.

## Escalation path

- In-app dispute flow via Ride history → Dispute ride charge.
