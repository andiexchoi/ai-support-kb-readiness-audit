## Source summary

Explains pending charges and authorization holds: holds are cancelled within a few business days, though some banks may take longer. Walks through possible sources for an unrecognized or unrefunded charge: a friend/family member using the account or payment method, a charge tied to a trip/order (updated fare, cancellation fee, tip), a recent cancellation, or an unresolved unknown charge that requires submitting details.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Authorization holds typically drop off within a few business days; bank may take longer.
- Cancellation fees can appear as charges (see UB-002).
- Does not give universal refund timing for all refund types.

## User action required

- Review possible explanations.
- If still unrecognized, share details via the form for support review.
