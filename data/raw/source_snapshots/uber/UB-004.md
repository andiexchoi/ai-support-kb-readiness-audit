# UB-004 — How is the price of a trip determined?

Company: Uber
URL: https://help.uber.com/en/riders/article/how-is-the-price-of-a-trip-determined?nodeId=d2d43bbc-f4bb-4882-b8bb-4bd8acf03a9d

## Source summary

Explains the inputs to Uber's upfront pricing: estimated trip time, distance, time of day, route, demand patterns, tolls, taxes, surcharges, and fees (excluding wait time fees). Lists the same upfront-price-change conditions as UB-003. Notes Uber may adjust trip payment post-trip in narrow cases (e.g., driver paid the cash toll rate). If the upfront price is not honored, the rider is charged the minimum or a time/distance-based price. Receipts explain any differences.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Upfront price may change for added stops, destination updates, significant route/duration changes, or unaccounted tolls.
- Post-trip payment adjustments are mentioned only in narrow cases.
- If upfront not honored: minimum price or measured time/distance price.

## User action required

- Review upfront price and receipt; receipt explains any difference.
