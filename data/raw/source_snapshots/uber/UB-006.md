# UB-006 — I think my account was hacked

Company: Uber
URL: https://help.uber.com/en/riders/article/i-think-my-account-was-hacked?nodeId=68905011-d5a0-4311-bd20-25c7dec719bf
Date accessed: 2026-06-05
Visible last updated date: Not visible

## Relevant issue families

- unexpected_or_disputed_charge (account-compromise branch)

## Source summary

Lists suspicious account activity signals: trip requests or completed trips the rider didn't make, unexpected driver calls/texts, unrecognized receipts, unexpected account or payment changes, password/email changes. If the rider can sign in, instructs them to reset their password. If they cannot sign in, or if they found charges on a bank account that don't appear on the Uber account, asks them to share email, phone, last 4 digits of the card, and details for support follow-up.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Signs of compromise are enumerated.
- Action paths differ based on whether the rider can sign in.

## User action required

- If signed in: Help > "I can't sign in or request a ride" > "I forgot my password."
- If not signed in: submit account details via the form.

## Escalation path

- Password reset, account recovery, support form follow-up.

## Notes

- Relevant for unrecognized-charge scenarios that may indicate account compromise.
- Pair with UB-001 (trip not taken refund) for the most common branches of "unrecognized charge."
