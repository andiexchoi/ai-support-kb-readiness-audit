## Source summary

Logged-in flow that asks the rider to select a trip and the date of the original charge to view how a previously requested refund was processed. The page itself is sparse and acts as a router into account-specific refund status rather than publishing a universal refund timeline.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Does not state a universal refund timeline; status is shown after trip selection.

## User action required

- Log in, select the trip and the date of the original charge.

## Escalation path

- Login + trip selection; downstream routes to support.
