## Source summary

Public help-center article that explains Uber's cancellation-fee rules, when fees do and do not apply, the difference between a cancellation fee and a temporary authorization hold, and how riders can request review of an incorrect cancellation fee or report a charge for a trip they did not take.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Cancellation fee timing varies by ride type:
  - Shared Rides: fee may apply if the rider cancels more than 2 minutes after acceptance, or if the driver cancels after waiting at least 2 minutes at pickup.
  - Economy Rides (UberX): more than 2 minutes after acceptance for rider; driver wait threshold is 7 minutes (10 minutes for Comfort).
  - Premium Rides (Black, BlackSUV): more than 5 minutes after acceptance for rider; driver wait threshold is 15 minutes.
- A cancellation fee **will not apply** if Uber detects the driver has not made progress to the pickup location, or if the driver is 5 or more minutes late.
- Cancellation fees vary by location and demand, and may reflect distance driven, time taken to arrive, and wait time.
- A grace period may apply to riders who cancel multiple trips in a short period.
- Wait-time fees and no-show windows start at the driver's GPS-reported arrival, which may not exactly match real-world coordinates.
- Uber periodically experiments with cancellation-policy changes; some cancellations may be waived for a randomly selected subset.
- What looks like a full-fare charge on a canceled trip may be a temporary authorization hold; Uber voids the hold immediately but it can take a few business days to clear depending on the bank.

## User action required

1. Determine whether the charge is a cancellation fee, full fare, or authorization hold.
2. If a cancellation fee feels incorrect, enter the trip date in the in-page form to submit for review.
3. If the rider did not take the trip, complete the linked "I was charged for a ride I did not take" form.

## Escalation path

- In-page review form for incorrect cancellation fees ("enter the date below and we'll get back to you").
- Linked form for "I was charged for a ride I did not take."
