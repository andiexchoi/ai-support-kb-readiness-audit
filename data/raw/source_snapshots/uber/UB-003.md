# UB-003 — Accepting a trip price

## Relevant issue families

## Source summary

Explains that requesting a ride accepts the upfront price shown. Upfront prices include rate for time/distance plus fees, tolls, and surcharges. Lists conditions under which the upfront price may change: adding stops, updating destination, significant route/duration changes, or a toll not factored in. Notes wait time and multi-stop fees. If no upfront price is available, the rider sees a price range and the trip is priced at destination.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Upfront price may change for: added stops, destination updates, significant route/duration changes, unaccounted tolls.
- Wait time fee grace period starts at driver's arrival; arrival time is based on GPS and may not perfectly match real-world coordinates.
- Does **not** state a dispute path for "driver took an inefficient route."

## User action required

- Review upfront price before requesting.
- If final price differs, the receipt explains why.

## Escalation path

- No dispute form on this page; downstream charge-help articles are referenced.
