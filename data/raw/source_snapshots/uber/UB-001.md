## Source summary

Public help-center page for riders who did not take a requested trip but were still charged. The page asks the rider to select a date or use the toggle to report the issue, then says Uber will get back to them. It links to Uber's refund policy and requires login to submit help.

## Relevant policy language summary

- The page acknowledges that a rider may have been charged for a trip they did not take and offers a submission flow.
- The page does **not** state decision rules for refund approval; eligibility logic lives elsewhere (e.g., UB-002, refund policy page).

## User action required

1. Open the help page.
2. Select the relevant trip date or use the toggle for "did not take the trip."
3. Log in and submit the issue.

## Escalation path

- Submission form on the page; Uber says it will get back to the rider.
- Refund policy link for additional context.
