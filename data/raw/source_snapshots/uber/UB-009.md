## Source summary

Describes how riders contact Uber support. For trip-specific issues: open Your Trips, select the trip, open Help, choose the issue category, submit feedback to 24/7 Customer Support. Replies arrive within ~24 hours via email and in-app push notification. Past support correspondence is visible in the app's Support messages section.

## Relevant policy language summary

- 24/7 support team; ~24 hour reply window.
- Trip-specific contact requires selecting the trip in Your Trips.

## User action required

- For a trip issue: Your Trips > select trip > Help > category > submit feedback.
- For non-trip issues: Help articles with in-article contact option.

## Escalation path

- Trip-specific Help flow.
- Email + push notification reply.
- In-app Support messages history.

## Notes

- Pair with UB-010, which states Uber does not offer a customer service number for riders.
