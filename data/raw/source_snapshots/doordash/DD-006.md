## Relevant issue families

## Source summary

Public help-center article explaining how to check refund and account-credit status. Distinguishes pending charges (typically drop off within a few business days) from posted-charge refunds (may take several business days to return to the original payment method). Describes how to view account credits, how to confirm a refund was issued, and when to contact support if a refund is past the expected window.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Pending charges typically drop off within a few business days.
- Posted-charge refunds may take several business days to return to the original payment method, depending on the bank or payment provider.
- Account credits are visible within the DoorDash account.

## User action required

- Check refund/credit status in the DoorDash app under Account/Orders.
- Compare pending vs posted timing against the article's windows.
- Contact support if the refund has not arrived within the stated window.

## Escalation path

- Routes to support if a refund does not arrive within the expected window.
