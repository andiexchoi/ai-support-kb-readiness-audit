# DD-002 — How can I report a missing or incorrect item from my order?

## Relevant issue families

## Source summary

Public help-center article explaining how to report missing or incorrect items in a DoorDash order. Distinguishes "missing" items (item not delivered) from "incorrect" or "wrong" items (item delivered but wrong). Walks the user through the in-app reporting flow under Orders > Help. States that resolutions may include account credit, refund, or redelivery depending on the situation. Photo evidence may be requested.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Resolutions "may include" account credit, refund, or redelivery — not a guaranteed outcome.
- Reports should be made shortly after receiving the order.
- Outcomes depend on the specific order and DoorDash review.

## User action required

1. Open the DoorDash app.
2. Go to Orders and select the affected order.
3. Tap Help.
4. Choose "Missing Items" or "Items Made Incorrectly."
5. Select the affected items and follow the prompts. Photo evidence may be requested.
