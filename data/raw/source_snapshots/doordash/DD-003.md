# DD-003 — Why was my order cancelled?

Company: DoorDash
URL: https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers/article/why-was-my-order-cancelled
Date accessed: 2026-06-05
Visible last updated date: Not visible

## Relevant issue families

- cancellation_or_refund

## Source summary

Public help-center article explaining common reasons DoorDash may cancel an order: store closed, items unavailable, Dasher unable to reach the store or customer, or a payment issue. States that when DoorDash cancels an order, the customer is not charged for items not delivered, and a refund will be issued for any charge that did post. Routes customers to refund-status guidance for follow-up.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Customers are not charged for items not delivered when DoorDash cancels the order.
- Refunds may take several business days to post depending on payment method.
- Explicit eligibility rules for *customer-initiated* cancellations are in DD-004, not this article.

## User action required

- If the order was cancelled by DoorDash, generally no further action is required to receive a refund for undelivered items.
- If a charge persists, check refund status (DD-006) or contact support.

## Escalation path

- Routes to refund-status article.
- Routes to support if a refund does not appear.
