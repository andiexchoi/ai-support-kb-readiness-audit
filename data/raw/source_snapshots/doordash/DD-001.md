# DD-001 — What can I do if I did not receive my order?

## Relevant issue families

## Source summary

Public help-center article that tells customers how to report an order that did not arrive. The article apologizes, then walks the user through reporting the issue from their DoorDash account on iOS, Android, or desktop. It says DoorDash investigates every reported never-delivered order and routes users to either select a resolution or chat with an agent. It also offers live chat in the app and a phone number.

## Relevant policy language summary

- DoorDash states it investigates reports of orders that were never delivered.
- The article does **not** publish explicit refund, credit, or redelivery eligibility rules.
- Final resolution happens inside the account/order flow ("Follow the prompts on the screen to either select a resolution or chat with an agent").

## User action required

1. Open the DoorDash app or desktop site.
2. Go to Orders and select the specific order.
3. Tap or click Help.
4. Under "Delivery Issues," select "Order never arrived."
5. Follow the prompts to select a resolution or chat with an agent.

## Escalation path

- In-app chat with an agent (after selecting "Order never arrived")
- Live chat via the DoorDash app
- Phone support: 855-431-0459
