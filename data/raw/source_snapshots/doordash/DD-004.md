# DD-004 — How do I cancel my order?

## Source summary

Public help-center article explaining how to cancel a DoorDash order and how the refund amount depends on order status at the time of cancellation. Provides a clear status-based rule set: full refund if the merchant has not yet confirmed and a Dasher has not been assigned; partial refund if the merchant has confirmed but a Dasher has not been assigned; no refund (and the food may still be delivered) if a Dasher has already been assigned and is en route.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Full refund: merchant not yet confirmed, Dasher not assigned.
- Partial refund: merchant confirmed, Dasher not yet assigned.
- No refund / food may still be delivered: Dasher assigned and en route.

## User action required

1. Open the DoorDash app.
2. Go to Orders and select the order.
3. Tap Help and choose "Cancel Order."
4. Follow prompts. If self-service cancellation is unavailable, contact support.

## Escalation path

- Routes to in-app cancellation flow.
- Routes to support if cancellation cannot be completed in-app.
