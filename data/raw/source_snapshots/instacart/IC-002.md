## Source summary

Help-center article explaining how customers report order issues — missing items, incorrect items, damaged/spoiled items, poor replacements, late orders, wrong-order delivered, and orders that never arrived. Covers app and website reporting steps and refund/credit conditions, including same-day refunds when reported within 24 hours.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Order issues must be **self-reported within 3 days** of pickup or delivery; each issue is reported separately.
- Not all issues automatically qualify for a credit or refund.
- Refund or credit may be up to the amount paid for the affected item; partial refund or credit may apply.
- Same-day refunds may be possible if the issue is reported within **24 hours** of receiving the order; they appear as an adjustment to the original charge.
- EBT SNAP orders are **not eligible for credit** — refund only; handled by Instacart Customer Experience.
- In-store returns may be required for some items (high-value or certified delivery from eligible retailers).
- Refunds for PayPal or Klarna payments are issued directly to those accounts.

## User action required

In the app:
1. Tap the Account icon.
2. Tap Orders.
3. Tap **Get help** on the relevant order.
4. Tap the issue you experienced; if an item issue, select the affected item(s).
5. Select your preferred resolution.

On the website:
1. Go to Instacart.com and click the 3 horizontal lines (top left).
2. Select **Your orders**.
3. Click **Report a problem** on the relevant order.
4. Select the issue; if an item issue, choose affected item(s).
5. Select your preferred resolution.

## Escalation path

- Log in for help routing.
- Instacart Customer Experience for EBT SNAP, OTC/Medicare card, or PayPal/Klarna refund issues.
