## Source summary

Comprehensive Instacart+ membership management article. Explains trial auto-enrollment into paid plans; charges may be applied up to 6 hours prior to renewal and are refundable per Instacart+ Terms. Covers updating payment method, plan changes, and cancellation steps. States that for ANNUAL members, the rider may cancel within the first 5 calendar days of the paid Instacart+ membership for a refund of the membership fee, only if they have not placed any orders using Instacart+. If canceled at any other time, no refund — but the user keeps $0 delivery fees through the end of the billing period.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Annual members: 5-calendar-day refund window if no orders used Instacart+ benefits.
- Otherwise: no refund; benefits continue through end of billing period.
- Charges may apply up to 6 hours before renewal and are refundable per Instacart+ Terms.

## User action required

1. Open Account icon / menu.
2. Select Your Instacart+ membership.
3. Under Manage your membership, select Cancel membership.
4. Confirm cancellation.
5. Watch for the cancellation confirmation email.
