# IC-003 — Problem with your order

Company: Instacart
URL: https://www.instacart.com/help/section/2151337978
Date accessed: 2026-06-06
Visible last updated date: Not visible

## Relevant issue families

- missing_delivery
- wrong_or_incomplete_order

## Source summary

Short summary article listing reportable order problems (missing items, incorrect items, damaged items, poor replacements, late orders) and what to expect after reporting (email confirmation for credits/refunds, refund/credit amounts vary). Explicitly states that if the order never arrived, the customer should reach out to Instacart Customer Experience.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Refund and credit amounts vary.
- Not all issues automatically qualify.
- For orders that never arrived, contact Instacart Customer Experience.

## User action required

- Report a problem via app or website.
- For never-arrived orders, contact Customer Experience directly.

## Escalation path

- "If your order never arrived, reach out to Instacart Customer Experience."
- Login for further help.
