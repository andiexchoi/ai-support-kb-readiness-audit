# IC-004 — View receipts and charges

## Relevant issue families

## Source summary

Strong source for understanding charges, receipt anatomy, and refund timing. Explains how to view a receipt, what each line item means (Original Charge, Additional Charge, Adjustment Charge, Refund for order issue, Total Charged), tip adjustments, temporary authorization holds, refund-not-showing scenarios, and how to dispute a charge by reporting an issue on the order.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Temporary authorization holds may appear higher than the estimated total and adjust once the order is complete.
- Same-day refunds appear as an adjustment to the original charge, not as a separate line item.
- Most refunds appear within 5–10 business days depending on financial institution.
- To dispute: Your orders > select order > Report an issue.

## User action required

1. Open receipt: app (Account > Orders > Receipt) or web (Your orders > Receipt).
2. Compare receipt line items to bank statement.
3. To dispute: Your orders > select order > Report an issue.

## Escalation path

- In-order Report an issue path.
- Login for further help.
