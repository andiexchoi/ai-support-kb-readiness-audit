## Source summary

Help-center article that explains Instacart's refund and return policy for grocery and other retailer orders. Customers can self-report order problems within 3 days of pickup or delivery (excluding restaurant orders) for a credit or refund. Missing or damaged items may be eligible for refund or credit up to the amount paid. Includes special handling for EBT SNAP and OTC Network/Medicare refunds and for PayPal/Klarna payments.

## Relevant policy language summary

- Customers can self-report problems within **3 days** of pickup or delivery, excluding restaurant orders.
- Reportable issues include: items missing from the final receipt and items that are damaged, spoiled, or otherwise unusable.
- Instacart may issue a **refund or credit** toward a future order **up to the amount paid** for an item.
- Partial refund or credit may apply if only part of an item is missing or damaged.
- High-value or certified-delivery items may require an in-store return at eligible retailers, depending on store policy.
- Refunds are subject to certain conditions.
- Same-day refunds appear as adjustments to the original charge rather than as a separate line.
- EBT SNAP and OTC/Medicare card refunds must be processed by Instacart Customer Experience, not the retailer.

## User action required

1. Open the order and self-report the problem within 3 days of pickup or delivery.
2. For missing items, report items that were charged on the final receipt but not received.

## Escalation path

- Customer Experience via the **Get Help** button for general issues.
- Direct contact with Instacart Customer Experience for EBT SNAP and OTC/Medicare card refunds.
- PayPal or Klarna support for issues with refunds returned to those accounts.
