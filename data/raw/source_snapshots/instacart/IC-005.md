# IC-005 — Unrecognized charge

Company: Instacart
URL: https://www.instacart.com/secure_forms/unrecognized_charges
Date accessed: 2026-06-06
Visible last updated date: Not visible

## Relevant issue families

- unexpected_or_disputed_charge

## Source summary

Secure form for submitting a fully unrecognized Instacart charge for review. Collects contact info, charge amount and date, card details (cardholder name, last 4 digits, card brand including PayPal/Klarna/EBT, card type, expiration, billing ZIP), and optional screenshots. Used when the charge cannot be tied to a known order or family/friend usage.

## Relevant policy language summary

- No public policy text on the form itself.
- Form-based intake for Instacart Customer Experience review.

## User action required

- Submit the form with required charge and card details.

## Escalation path

- Form-based intake.
- Downstream Instacart Customer Experience review.

## Notes

- Pair with IC-004 to differentiate "I understand my receipt but a line is wrong" (IC-004) from "I do not recognize this charge at all" (IC-005).
